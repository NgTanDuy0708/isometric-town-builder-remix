# Delivery truck and order board

Generation mode: built-in image_gen, transparent RGBA. Original generated PNGs are retained byte for byte. The truck's animated sheets use four native-resolution frames per direction; source frames are cropped only at render time.

## Truck design

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. High-resolution richly detailed pixel art asset for an isometric medieval fantasy farming-town game. Strict 2:1 dimetric orthographic camera, upper-left light, fine crisp pixel clusters, clean dark outlines, warm oak and antique brass, russet-red accents, muted natural colours. Native original detail, no coarse mosaic or blur, not glossy 3D/painterly. True transparent RGBA background, no ground diamond, landscape, cast shadow, text, watermark, logo, comparison labels or painted checkerboard. Isolate the complete subject with at least 7% transparent margin on every side. Square canvas.
Create ONE new finished ROYAL STEAM DELIVERY TRUCK for the village. One charming compact four-wheeled early steam-powered cargo automobile, NO horse, no wagon shafts or hitch. The vehicle's FRONT NOSE FACES DOWN-LEFT / lower-left on screen, along a +row road direction, and its rear cargo bed extends up-right. Show the front grille, near SIDE and cargo bed in a coherent 3/4 isometric view.
A dark honey-brown oak flatbed with low side rails and iron corner brackets, burgundy red cab side panels, antique golden brass hood with rounded radiator grille, two small warm round headlights, modest short black chimney, exposed short brass side pipe. Small OPEN driver seating area with steering wheel, no driver. Four dark wood spoked wheels with small brass hubs and iron tyres; exactly two wheels are clearly visible on the near side, and far wheels only if physically visible. Do not obscure the wheel rims with cargo.
Carry a tidy readable load: three small wooden crates, two tied burlap sacks, one closed barrel, tied down with one coherent rope. Keep cargo low enough for a clean silhouette. Truck is substantial but compact and friendly; full wheels touch one flat road plane with a clear bottom wheel-contact anchor. The roof/cab/cargo do not clip. No exhaust smoke in the PNG, no speed streaks, no people, no animals. This one intact vehicle will be the style/design reference for an animated spritesheet.
```
