# Stone quarry workshop — three upgrade levels

Mode: built-in image_gen, transparent background. Building ID: `b65_stone_quarry`. Original generated PNGs retained without resizing or palette reduction. Lv2 and Lv3 use the preceding generated image as a continuity reference; the separate stone resource node remains unchanged.

## Delivered assets

- Lv1: `public/assets/buildings/stone_quarry_lv1.png`
- Lv2: `public/assets/buildings/stone_quarry_lv2.png`
- Lv3: `public/assets/buildings/stone_quarry_lv3.png`
- Originals and measured geometry: `assets-source/stone-quarry/`

All images are 1254x1254 RGBA with true transparency, retained byte for byte. Native geometry is registered in buildingSprites.js; the existing world-object scale is applied by the renderer. The stone quarry now uses the three level-specific PNGs instead of the provisional SVG. Existing workforce, recipes, stock, and upgrade costs are retained.

Continuity instructions used with Lv2: edit the preceding Lv1 workshop into its Lv2 upgrade, preserving the art family, orientation and compact footprint. For Lv3, edit Lv2 into the highest upgrade, add the loft and stone arches, and keep all lifting equipment and suspended cargo entirely within the frame with transparent margins.
